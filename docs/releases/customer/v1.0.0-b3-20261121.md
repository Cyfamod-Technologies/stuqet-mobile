### Summary

Customer and rider screens built from the approved designs and run on bundled demo data.

### User-Facing Changes

- Onboarding: intro slides, welcome, create account with email or phone verification, role picker (order food or deliver), school, food types, address, notifications, fingerprint and "You're all set".
- Customer: Home, Search with filters, vendor menus, dish with size and add-ons, Cart with coupon and notes, Checkout and delivery addresses, Order placed, Orders, live order tracking with a map, chat with the vendor, order rating, Profile, Notifications and Appearance (light, dark or match the phone).
- Rider: application (profile, vehicle, documents, payout) and under review, Home dashboard, Deliveries by status, delivery detail with picked up, on the way and delivered steps, cancellation request, Earnings, Vehicle and documents, Payout details.
- One login for both roles; a rider can switch to ordering food and a customer can become a rider from the Profile.

### Bug Fixes

- Sign-in and password reset now use the customer designs (email or phone, reset signs the person in).

### Security, Privacy, and Compliance

### Testing Scope

- Walk the ordering loop (Home, vendor, dish, cart, checkout, placed, track) and the rider loop (Deliveries, detail, confirm delivery) in demo mode with `npm run dev:customer:demo`.
- Check empty, loading and error states with the New customer account and the demo "no connection" switch.
- Check light and dark theme on every new screen.

### Known Issues

- The food catalogue, cart, order, chat and rider application endpoints do not exist in the backend yet (marked `TODO(backend)` in each feature's `api.ts`); the app runs on demo data until they do.
- Online payment is treated as paid in demo mode; Paystack is not wired for food orders.
- Push notification permission and Google and Apple sign-in are not wired.
